About Us

Passionate about the quality of aircraft maintenance

We offer tailor-made maintenance solutions for General Aviation professionals and enthusiasts. Our certified mechanics perform annual inspections, general maintenance, and repairs — on your schedule, at your hangar.

Over 30 years of combined experience, certified A&P/IA mechanics on staff, and a specialization in mobile maintenance. Veteran owned and operated.

Based out of Monroe County Airport in Madisonville, Tennessee, we bring the shop to the aircraft. That means less repositioning, less downtime, and a mechanic who actually knows your airplane.

What We Do

Services

Inspections, maintenance, and repairs for piston General Aviation aircraft — performed on site whenever the job allows.

Annual Inspections

Complete annual inspections signed off by an IA, with findings documented and discussed before any work proceeds.

100-Hour Inspections

Scheduled 100-hour inspections for aircraft operated for hire or flight instruction, kept on cycle so you stay legal and flying.

General Maintenance & Repairs

Unscheduled squawks, component replacement, troubleshooting, and the routine upkeep that keeps an airframe healthy between inspections.

Mobile / On-Site Service

Our specialty. We bring tooling and expertise to your hangar or tiedown so your aircraft doesn't have to be flown somewhere to be worked on.

AD Research & Compliance

Airworthiness Directive research, compliance verification, and clean recordkeeping so the status of your aircraft is never in question.

Logbook Entries & Return to Service

Accurate, legible logbook entries and return-to-service documentation that hold up at the next annual — and at resale.
